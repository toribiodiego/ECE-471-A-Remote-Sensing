## Drought Forecasting in California’s Central Valley


### Objective  
Our goal is to forecast drought conditions up to 12 weeks ahead in California’s Central Valley using remote sensing and machine learning. We begin by replicating the [*DroughtCast*](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/big-data/articles/10.3389/fdata.2021.773478/full) framework to build a baseline dataset and then enrich it with satellite imagery, soil moisture, weather, and water storage data via Google Earth Engine. Finally, we train and validate a GRU-based sequence-to-sequence model to deliver early drought warnings that help improve decision-making in agriculture, water management, and environmental planning.
